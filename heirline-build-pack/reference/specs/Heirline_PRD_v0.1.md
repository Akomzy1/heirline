## 1. Summary

Heirline builds and maintains a **verified register of everything a Nigerian family owns**. That covers land, listed shares, unclaimed dividends, dormant bank accounts, pensions, insurance, private-company stakes, offshore holdings and informal loans. Heirline then keeps the register current, protected and ready for the day the family needs it.

Heirline does **not** write wills, hold assets, act as trustee or give investment advice. Those layers are crowded and regulated. Heirline is the **independent discovery, verification and monitoring layer** that sits behind the will. Partner law firms and trustee companies act on its output.

**Tagline (brand):** *Verified. Protected. Ready.*

**One-liner (partners and positioning):** *The verified asset register behind your will.*

**Starting product (wedge):** the **Land & Paper Health Check**, a fixed-scope, fixed-fee audit. It produces a verified register, a title-risk score per parcel, a prioritised title-perfection plan and a list of recoverable funds.

## 2. Problem

Wealthy Nigerian families typically hold assets that are **scattered, under-documented and known in full only to the principal**. When the principal dies or loses capacity:

- the family cannot find all the assets;
- titles turn out to be imperfect;
- probate stalls;
- land gets encroached;
- relatives litigate.

### Evidence base

| Signal | Evidence | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Unclaimed dividends are large and growing | SEC put unclaimed dividends at ₦242bn (June 2025). The SEC's July 2026 recovery campaign cited about ₦270bn. A registrar executive cited about ₦200bn (Mar 2026). | High (quote as a ₦200bn–₦270bn range) |
| Dormant bank balances move into a government-held pool | CBN 2024 guidelines move balances dormant 10+ years into the Unclaimed Balances Trust Fund. Four banks published 321,181 dormant accounts (May 2026). | High on the rule. Aggregate value unpublished. |
| Policy is moving these funds further away | Finance Act 2020 Unclaimed Funds Trust Fund. In Aug 2026 the FEC approved directing unclaimed dividends and dormant funds to NELFUND, subject to the governing laws. Owners retain their legal claim. | High. Procedure still evolving. |
| Land is mostly unregistered | Housing Minister (Mar 2025): over 90% of land in Nigeria is unregistered. The FG–World Bank programme (Sep 2024) targets mass titling. | High |
| Perfecting titles has become much more expensive | Lagos Governor's Consent costs about 3% of assessed value. The 2026 Blue Book (effective 1 May 2026) raised values by up to about 300% in prime areas. Projected consent fees in Lekki Phase 1 are ₦40m–₦90m. | Medium-high |
| Encroachment is chronic, and enforcement is slow | The Lagos land-grabbers task force received 1,628 petitions (May 2023–Apr 2026) and concluded 221. | High |
| Even well-advised wealthy estates stall for years | Lagos High Court (Aug 2026) appointed the Administrator-General as interim administrator of a billionaire's estate six years after death, amid a challenge to the will; defendants argued the estate's properties were "not sufficiently identified", and unaccounted rental income was alleged. (Internal evidence only: do not cite by name in marketing.) | High (public court record) |
| Intestacy is widespread | "70% die intestate" is widely cited but has no traceable primary source. | **Low.** Use as directional only. |

### Why now

- **Policy tailwinds:** NELFUND, the UFTF and dormant-account transfers create urgency to locate and claim assets.
- **Cost tailwinds:** the Blue Book fee rises force HNIs to *prioritise* which titles to perfect, which is a paid decision.
- **Digitisation:** Lagos e-GIS and the LAGIS law make discovery faster.
- **Supply:** no competitor sells verified, cross-geography asset discovery as a service (see §3).

## 5. Positioning, principles and non-goals

### Principles

1. **Non-custodial:** never hold client money, assets or original documents.
2. **Lawyers draft, Heirline discovers:** all land, probate and testamentary instruments go to partner law firms.
3. **Security over convenience:** a complete family asset map is a kidnapping and fraud target. Compartmentalise everything.
4. **Evidence over assertion:** every register line carries its evidence and a verification status.
5. **Deterministic core, AI at the edges:** risk scores and release triggers are rules-based and config-driven. AI extracts and matches. A human verifies.
6. **Fail closed:** if a check, trigger or access condition is ambiguous, nothing is released or published.
7. **Quiet brand:** sold through relationships, not advertised as an "AI product".

### Non-goals (v0.1)

- Will drafting, trust deeds, probate filings, or land instruments (partners do these).
- Acting as trustee or executor, or holding funds (trustee minimum capital is now ₦2bn).
- Investment, tax-planning or wealth-management advice.
- Running household-staff vetting, payroll or security operations (refer to a vetting partner).
- A mass-market consumer app.

## 6. Products and scope

### 6.1 Land & Paper Health Check (wedge, MVP)

**Scope:** up to 5 land parcels, plus all listed-share holdings, known bank accounts, pension (RSA) and life policies. Additional parcels are priced per unit.

**Deliverables:**
- Verified asset register (PDF report plus structured data).
- Title-risk score and status per parcel, with the evidence behind it.
- Prioritised title-perfection plan with indicative costs, executed by a partner law firm.
- Recoverable-funds list: unclaimed dividends, dormant accounts, unregistered share mandates.
- Gaps-and-exposure memo: missing documents, undocumented assets, informal loans.
- Verification statement signed by the engaged professional, framed as agreed-upon-procedures findings and not an audit opinion.

**Price hypothesis:** ₦3m–₦10m, banded by parcel count and geographies. Diaspora clients US$3k–$10k.

### 6.2 Continuity Retainer (recurring)

- Quarterly register refresh and change log.
- **Parcel monitoring:** periodic satellite change detection plus field verification on alert.
- **Regulatory watch:** dormant-account publications, UFTF/NELFUND procedure, registrar mandate status and Lagos fee schedules, matched against the client's assets.
- **Sealed family vault** with trigger-based release (§7.9).
- Annual "if I died tomorrow" drill.
- **Will health check (with partner law firm):** each year the partner law firm confirms the will exists, was properly executed and witnessed, is lodged at the Probate Registry (or where the original is held), reflects the current register, and that the named executors are still willing and able to act. Heirline records the outcome and flags gaps; it never drafts or opines on the will itself.
- **Insider exposure review:** who in the household can see documents, keys, or travel and asset information, with recommendations. Vetting is referred out.

**Price hypothesis:** ₦1.5m–₦5m per year.

### 6.3 Heir Readiness (add-on)

- **Staged disclosure plan** (the principal controls the level per heir; §7.11).
- A facilitated family briefing session.
- Hometown and key-parcel site walks.
- Executor day-one pack rehearsal.
- Family governance charter, drafted by a partner law firm and coordinated by Heirline.

**Price hypothesis:** ₦2m–₦6m per family. Validate.

### 6.4 Estate Asset Trace (post-death)

For executors and administrators whose estate is already in probate, sold through probate lawyers and trustees. Delivers a full discovery and verified schedule for the probate application and recovery claims.

**Price hypothesis:** fixed fee ₦1.5m–₦5m. A capped success component is possible only if legal advice confirms it is permissible.

### 6.5 White-label audit (V1+)

Trustee companies and private banks offer the Health Check under their own brand. Heirline is paid a wholesale per-audit fee or a revenue share.

## 8. AI agents

Agents are the **margin engine**, not the product. Every agent follows four rules:
- **Least-privilege data slice:** no agent sees a full family map.
- **Generator/examiner separation.**
- **No autonomous external action:** agents draft and humans send.
- **Zero-retention model endpoints only.**

| Agent | Job | Data access | Human checkpoint | Phase |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Intake Extractor** | Classify documents; extract structured fields from scans | Single document at a time | Examiner pass; mismatches go to analyst | MVP |
| **Examiner** | Independently re-verify extracted fields against the source; flag inconsistencies across documents (e.g. a name mismatch between deed and C of O) | Document + extracted fields | Analyst resolves flags | MVP |
| **Discovery Matcher** | Fuzzy-match identity variants against published dormant lists and registrar responses; explain each match | Identity variants + public lists (no other assets) | Analyst confirms each match | MVP |
| **Title Analyst** | Summarise parcel evidence into the rules engine's inputs; draft the narrative for the perfection plan | One parcel's evidence | Rules engine scores; analyst and professional review | MVP |
| **Regulatory Watch** | Monitor regulator and state sources; summarise changes; tag affected asset types | Public sources + pseudonymised asset-type index | Analyst approves before any client alert | V1 |
| **Parcel Monitor** | Triage imagery change detection (computer vision, not an LLM) and draft the alert | Pseudonymised parcel geometry | Field verification before the client is notified | V1 |
| **Case Ops** | Track recovery cases, draft follow-up letters for review, chase SLAs, assemble the day-one pack | Case-level slice | Human sends all external communications | V2 |
| **Heir Guide** | Answer heir questions within their disclosure level only | Content at heir's level (never above it) | Logged; principal-configurable | V2 |

**Model tiering (from the standard stack):**
- Haiku for classification and triage.
- Sonnet for extraction, matching rationale and summaries.
- Opus for the examiner role on high-value or conflicting evidence.

## 9. Security and privacy

### Threat model

| Threat | Example | Control |
|---|---|---|
| Kidnapping / extortion targeting | Leaked register reveals wealth and locations | Compartmentalised data, sealed vault, minimal retention, pseudonymised monitoring |
| Insider (Heirline staff) | Analyst exfiltrates a client map | Need-to-know slices; no single staff role sees the full map; access logging and alerts; staff vetting and NDAs |
| Insider (household) | Staff or relative accesses documents | Insider exposure review; content-free notifications |
| False-death fraud | Forged death certificate to trigger release | Multi-party attestation + cooling-off period + principal notification + fail closed |
| Coercion of the principal | Forced to unlock the vault | Key split (principal alone cannot release to third parties); duress code (V2) |
| Breach of a vendor or the platform | Cloud or database compromise | Client-side encryption for sealed data; field-level encryption for the working register; key management separated from data |
| Messaging leakage | Asset details sent over WhatsApp | Policy plus technical block: no asset data in notifications |

### Data lifecycle

- **SEC-1** The working register exists only during an active engagement or retainer.
- **SEC-2** At sign-off, the register is sealed into the vault (V1). The working copy is reduced to what monitoring needs, held under pseudonymised IDs.
- **SEC-3** Personally identifying data is stored separately from asset data and linked only by internal keys.
- **SEC-4** Product analytics (PostHog) receive no personal or asset data.
- **SEC-5** Role-based access with row-level security. Every read of sensitive fields is logged.
- **SEC-6** Working copies of scans are deleted on a defined schedule after delivery, unless the client opts into retention under the retainer.

### NDPA / GAID compliance

- Register with the NDPC as a data controller of major importance and appoint a DPO.
- Complete a **DPIA before the first client** (large-scale sensitive processing).
- Explicit consent for sensitive data, with a consent register.
- 72-hour breach notification to the NDPC, and immediate notice to clients where risk is high.
- Document the lawful basis for any cross-border transfer (cloud hosting, model APIs). Confirm the hosting region.
- Exposure: fines up to the greater of ₦10m or 2% of annual gross revenue.

### 11.1 Governance

**Statutory board (at launch):** Tokunbo as sole director. CAMA 2020 appears to allow a single director for a small private company or a single-shareholder company, and to exempt small companies from appointing a company secretary. Confirm with the company secretarial agent at registration.

**Advisory board (from month 0–3).** Trust is the product, so credible names matter more than legal structure. Advisers have no executive authority.

| Seat | Profile | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Legal | Senior estate or property lawyer (SAN or private-client partner) | Legal credibility; keeps the model clear of reserved legal work (LPA s.22) |
| Wealth channel | Former trustee company or private banking executive | Knows the buyers; opens the trustee and banker channel |
| Security and privacy | Cybersecurity and data protection specialist | Makes the security model credible; oversees NDPA posture |
| Land | Senior registered surveyor or former land registry official | Title and charting credibility |

**Adviser terms:**
- Compensation: a small equity grant (vesting over time) or a modest retainer.
- Signed NDA and conflict-of-interest declaration.
- **No access to client data, registers or the vault.** Advisers see anonymised aggregates only.
- Quarterly meeting with minutes; the advisers' names are used in pitch materials only with their written consent.

**Independence rule:** the engaged verifying professional (Oludayo at launch) is **not** a director or adviser while she signs verification statements. Her independence is part of what clients pay for and must withstand challenge by a sceptical client or a court. Revisit if her role changes.

**Triggers for a formal governance board:**
1. Outside investment is taken.
2. A white-label deal with a trustee company or bank requires governance in its due diligence.
3. Client volume makes a **client-protection and ethics committee** necessary. That committee would oversee staff data access, vault release decisions, trigger disputes and incident response, and include at least one independent member.

## 12. Data model (outline)

- **Family** → Principal, Persons (spouse, heirs, executors), Engagements
- **Person** → identity variants (aliases, maiden names, historic addresses); stored separately from asset data
- **Asset** → type, description, owner of record, value band, verification status
- **Parcel** (extends Asset) → state, LGA, geometry (PostGIS), title documents, charting result, risk score + rule version
- **EvidenceItem** → document reference, page, extracted fields, examiner result
- **Finding** → gap, risk or recoverable item; linked assets
- **RecoveryCase** → item, owner, status, SLA, outcome value
- **PartnerWorkPacket** → partner, scope, assets, status
- **Verification** → preparer, signer, checklist, statement version
- **Vault** → sealed version, key-share holders, release map
- **TriggerEvent** → type, attestations, cooling-off timer, outcome
- **AccessGrant** → person, disclosure level, scope, expiry
- **MonitoringEvent** → pseudonymised parcel or holding ID, source, detection, triage outcome

## 14. Key user flows

### 14.1 Health Check (MVP)

1. Referral from a partner or banker → discovery call.
2. Engagement letter, NDPA consent and letters of authority signed; fee paid.
3. Memory-prompts interview with the principal; document collection.
4. Intake → extraction → examiner pass → analyst resolution.
5. Discovery: dormant-list matching, registrar searches, land searches and charting.
6. Title-risk scoring and perfection plan; recoverable-funds list.
7. The engaged professional reviews and signs.
8. Report walkthrough with the principal and, optionally, their lawyer or trustee.
9. Partner work packets issued; recovery cases opened.
10. Offer the Continuity Retainer and Heir Readiness.

### 14.2 Trigger release (V1)

1. Executor or lawyer reports the death and submits the certificate.
2. Lawyer attestation recorded → cooling-off period starts → notices go to the principal's registered channels.
3. With no contrary signal and attestations complete, key shares combine and the release map executes.
4. Executor receives the day-one pack; heirs receive their scoped views; partners receive work packets.
5. Any conflict → fail closed → human review.

### 14.3 Estate Asset Trace

1. Probate lawyer refers the family → engagement with the administrator.
2. Discovery runs on the deceased's identity variants and documents.
3. Verified schedule delivered for the probate filing; recovery cases opened with the law firm.

## 18. Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Severity | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| A centralised asset map becomes a target | Very high | §9 controls; seal and minimise; no full-map role |
| HNIs won't disclose everything | High | Sell through trusted bankers and lawyers; start with land and papers; independent professional sign-off; test in the first 20 calls |
| Crossing into reserved legal work | High | Partner-only instruments; engagement-letter wording; legal review of templates |
| NDPA liability | High | DPIA, DPO, consent register, breach playbook, insurance |
| Partner dependence | Medium | Make partners money; keep the verified register as Heirline's owned layer |
| Long sales cycle (aversion to will-making) | Medium-high | Frame around protection and heirs, not death; trigger-based outreach; Trace offers immediate demand |
| Marketing that references live estate disputes | Medium | Never name families or comment on ongoing (sub judice) cases in public content; write about patterns; reference public court records only in private partner conversations |
| Pricing too high for the naira-wealthy | Medium | Banded scope; start small (5 parcels); retainer instalments |
| Professional bottleneck | Medium | Recruit a second professional by month 6 |
| Weak credibility at launch (single director, new brand) | Medium-high | Advisory board seated within the first 90 days (§11.1); names used with consent |
| Imagery cost or resolution doesn't work for small plots | Medium | Pilot one provider on 20 parcels; fall back to scheduled field checks |
