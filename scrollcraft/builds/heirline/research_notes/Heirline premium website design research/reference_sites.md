# Reference Sites for a Premium Heirline Marketing Site (private banks, wealth/trust, family offices, law/notaries, maisons, award winners)

Research date: 2026-11-11. Method: live fetches of the sites (text/markup extraction only, so no visual motion, CSS fonts or rendered imagery could be seen) plus Awwwards winner pages (credits, dates, scores, tags, featured-element names). Anything not seen directly is in Gaps, not stated as fact.

## 1. Which private banks, family offices and wealth/trust firms have the most refined websites?

### Takeaway
The big incumbent private banks (Pictet, Lombard Odier, Rothschild & Co, C. Hoare & Co) mostly run calm, heritage-led corporate sites with a slogan hero, key figures, a history module and an insights feed. The most refined patterns for Heirline are Pictet's one-question hero with archival history, Hoare's "since 1672" family statement, and Rothschild's restrained ambient video with full-width menu panels. None of them is a recent award winner. The award-recognised finance and family-office sites are boutiques: Yamauchi No.10 Family Office (SOTD 2021) and Haute Capital Partners (HM 2023).

### Cited Findings

**Pictet (pictet.com), Geneva, since 1805**
- Hero is a single question plus a one-line follow-up and one CTA: "How do you like your calm?" / "Before, during or after the storm?" / "Find out more" (links to /about/calm) — [Pictet](https://www.pictet.com/)
- Header is minimal: logo, a "Change region" selector that opens a "Select website" list grouped by Americas / Asia Pacific / Europe / Middle East, and "Client login" — [Pictet](https://www.pictet.com/)
- Section rhythm runs from the hero to "Who we are" (independent partnership, long-term outlook, since 1805), then a four-stat band (1,008 bn USD AUM/custody at 30 Sep 2026; 21.9% total capital ratio; 172% liquidity coverage ratio; 5,600+ FTE), then "Annual Review 2025" / "Since 1805", then "220 years of Pictet" with eight pivotal moments in archival images ("Explore our history"), then a timeline of Geneva addresses by year (1805, 1856, 1878, 1910…) — [Pictet](https://www.pictet.com/)
- Tone is measured and reassuring, with the calm/storm metaphor and words like permanence and responsibility — [Pictet](https://www.pictet.com/)

**C. Hoare & Co (hoaresbank.co.uk), London, since 1672**
- Header has a logo with standard and white variants (a sign of light/dark header switching over hero media), a "MENU" toggle and "LOGIN". The menu has five items: Customers, About Us, Philanthropy, News, Contact — [C. Hoare & Co](https://www.hoaresbank.co.uk/)
- Hero has the kicker "Private Banking", the headline "Expertise, empathy and exceptional flexibility" and a "SCROLL" cue — [C. Hoare & Co](https://www.hoaresbank.co.uk/)
- Sections in order: "Our thinking" (a carousel of six dated cards with reading time, including "Alexander's Summer Letter 2026", a recurring letter from the senior partner), then "A family business since 1672" (UK's oldest privately owned bank, Hoare family for 12 generations), then "Services tailored to fit you perfectly" with a Personal/Business toggle, then Get in touch (phone, email), app badges and a three-column footer (Company / Useful links / Site tools) — [C. Hoare & Co](https://www.hoaresbank.co.uk/)
- Tone is formal yet warm: "Customers are the heart of our business, and they always will be" — [C. Hoare & Co](https://www.hoaresbank.co.uk/)

**Rothschild & Co (rothschildandco.com)**
- Hero is a muted looping ambient video of people moving through a glass revolving door in a lobby, with a still fallback. Headline: "One of the world's leading independent financial services groups" — [Rothschild & Co](https://www.rothschildandco.com/en/)
- Navigation: Global Advisory, Wealth and Asset Management, Five Arrows and About us open full-width panels with "Back"/"Close menu" controls and grouped links under uppercase labels ("WHO WE ARE", "OPPORTUNITIES"). **Lombard Odier (lombardodier.com), since 1796**
- Uses a lowercase-with-full-stop heading system throughout ("about us.", "private banking.", "where we are.", "get in touch.") and the trademark line "rethink everything®" — [Lombard Odier](https://www.lombardodier.com/)
- The hero is a five-slide rotating editorial carousel ("The new map of capital", "Freedom"…), followed by a "watch our manifesto" video. Stat tiles follow ("+230 years", "+200 investment professionals", "+25 offices"), then a headquarters block with directions and an on-page contact form — [Lombard Odier](https://www.lombardodier.com/)

### Inferences
- These banks share a hero pattern: one short claim, one CTA, no product grid. Their proof device is a sober figure band (AUM, capital ratios, years) right after the first statement. For Heirline the equivalent is process proof rather than AUM: registers built, titles checked, a 2-of-3 seal, independent chartered-accountant sign-off.
- Hoare's "Alexander's Summer Letter" (a recurring dated letter from the senior partner) is a direct model for Heirline's calm senior-partner voice: a dated "Letter from the Partner" in place of a blog.
- Pictet's history module (eight moments, archival images) and address timeline show heritage told as a ledger: dated entries in a list, not marketing copy. This maps naturally onto a "register" metaphor.
- Rothschild's ambient lobby video is the closest to restraint-with-motion, but it features people. Heirline's brief forbids people in suits, so the analogue would be an object-led loop (ledger pages, a seal, brass hardware, a survey plan).
- No major private bank holds a recent SOTD. A Heirline site at award level would beat the category on craft, not just match it.

### Gaps
- Typefaces, colours, hover states, scroll motion and transitions could not be seen for any bank site, because WebFetch returns text, not rendered CSS/JS. Treat the motion descriptions above as structural only.
- Julius Baer, Coutts, Edmond de Rothschild and Bessemer Trust were not fetched (tool budget). Patek Philippe and A. Lange & Söhne were not seen either: alange-soehne.com returned HTTP 403 to the fetcher. All remain candidates to review in a browser.

## 2. Which luxury maisons, watchmakers and premium studios use scroll storytelling well, and how?

### Takeaway
The best-documented recent luxury scroll piece is Cartier's Watches & Wonders 2025 microsite (Awwwards SOTD, Immersive Garden + 60fps). It uses WebGL "3D universes" navigated by scroll, product interaction, and sound design, and scored 9.00 for Animations/Transitions. Main maison sites (e.g. patek.com) are more conservative: product catalogues with a topline video.

### Cited Findings
- **Cartier Watches & Wonders 2025**: Awwwards SOTD 18 Aug 2025. Credits: Immersive Garden, 60fps, mooders (music & sound design). SOTD 7.64 (Design 7.84, Usability 7.17, Creativity 7.97, Content 7.56); Dev 7.55 with Animations/Transitions 9.00, Accessibility 6.80. Tags: Luxury, Scrolling, 3D, WebGL, Gestures/Interaction, Motion. Featured elements: "Content Pages", "3D Universes" (Scroll category), "Products Interaction". Described as "a journey through refined universes showcasing iconic timepieces" — [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/cartier-watches-wonders-2025)
- **Patek Philippe** relaunched patek.com ("Version 6") on 2 Oct 2017 for the site's 20th year, mobile-first because over 50% of users were on smartphones — [Patek Philippe press release](https://static.patek.com/pdf/pressreleases/es/2017_PatekPhilippe_New_PatekCom.pdf). A UK agency review criticised the homepage for having few interactions beyond a topline video — [KIJO](https://kijo.london/blog/luxury-watch-websites). WorldTempus noted prices placed beneath a scroll button — [WorldTempus](https://en.worldtempus.com/article/patek-philippe-fresh-and-clean--the-new-patekcom-24803.html)

### Inferences
- The Cartier scores show the trade-off: award juries reward motion (9.00), while usability (7.17) and accessibility (6.80) lag. For Heirline, whose audience is older HNW principals and their advisers, one signature scroll sequence (e.g. the vault's 2-of-3 seal assembling) inside an otherwise quiet, readable document page beats a fully immersive WebGL universe.
- Patek's restraint (catalogue plus one film) is itself a luxury signal. It shows heritage brands deliberately under-animate.

### Gaps
- Hermès, Aesop, Loro Piana and Vacheron Constantin sites, plus premium architecture and real-estate studios, were not fetched or verified this round (tool budget). No claims are made about their current UI. Recommended for direct browser study.
- No agency case study (Locomotive, Immersive Garden, Studio Freight/darkroom, Resn) was read in full. Immersive Garden's credit on Cartier is confirmed only via Awwwards.

## 3. Which Awwwards/FWA SOTD winners (2023–2026) in finance, legal, luxury or heritage are relevant, and what techniques do they use?

### Takeaway
There are few verified finance/legal winners in 2023–2026. The relevant set: Cartier W&W 2025 (SOTD, luxury, WebGL scroll), Kümmerlein Lawyers & Notaries (HM Jun 2025, typography-led "law firm without clichés"), Haute Capital Partners (HM 2023, family office, data-viz and animated header) and Integrated Wealthcare (HM Nov 2025). Older but relevant: Yamauchi No.10 Family Office (SOTD 2021, storytelling with page transitions) and Quincy Réquin (SOTD 2014, minimal, unusual navigation).

### Cited Findings
- **Kümmerlein Lawyers & Notaries** (kuemmerlein.de): Awwwards HM 18 Jun 2025 by Kitto Katsu Branding. Described as "A law firm site without the usual clichés", "clear, warm, and confident", built around the promise "To make it simple, but significant." Tags: Clean, Typography, Graphic design, UI design. Palette: #F7F788 (pale yellow). Featured elements: UI element based on identity, Team, Bespoke icons. Community overall scores ranged 6.40–8.80 — [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/kummerlein-lawyers-notaries)
- **Quincy Réquin & Associates** (Lyon/Paris law firm): SOTD 14 Dec 2014 by Rezo Zero, 7.33. Tags: Minimal, Flat, Clean, Unusual Navigation. Palette: #FF9398, #9C9C9C, #FFFFFF — [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/quincy-requin-associates-lawyers)
- **PXR Legal**: SOTD (7.26), about 2020 — [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/pxr-legal)
- Cartier W&W 2025, Yamauchi No.10 and Haute Capital: see sections 1–2 for scores and techniques — [Cartier](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/cartier-watches-wonders-2025); [Yamauchi](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/yamauchi-no-10-family-office); [Haute Capital](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/haute-capital-partners)

### Inferences
- Kümmerlein is the closest analogue to Heirline's positioning: a notary/legal brand winning on typography, a bespoke icon system and identity-derived UI elements rather than WebGL. A brass hairline icon set (title deed, seal, key, ledger, signature) is the Heirline equivalent.
- Yamauchi's "road movie about the family" framing, with page transitions as a featured element, supports a chaptered family narrative (register, titles, vault, sign-off, heirs) told as sequential scenes.

### Gaps
- FWA and CSSDA winner pages were not checked (budget). No 2023–2026 FWA/CSSDA finance or legal winners are confirmed here.
- No 2023–2026 Awwwards SOTD (as opposed to HM) for a private bank, trust company or notary was found. The search results showed none, but the Awwwards archive filters were not exhaustively browsed.

## 5. Recurring patterns among the best, and what separates luxury restraint from template-feel

### Takeaway
The best sites lead with one sentence and one action, prove themselves with dated or numbered facts, tell heritage as a dated record, keep navigation to around five items with full-width panels, and save motion for one or two signature moments. Template-feel comes from carousels of competing hero slides, stock people, card grids of equal weight, and generic CTAs.

### Cited Findings
- One-line hero with a single CTA: Pictet ("How do you like your calm?" / "Find out more") and Hoare ("Expertise, empathy and exceptional flexibility" with a "SCROLL" cue) — [Pictet](https://www.pictet.com/); [Hoare](https://www.hoaresbank.co.uk/)
- Dated or numbered proof bands: Pictet's four stats with "as at" dates; Rothschild's 694 transactions / €150bn / 4,800 colleagues; Lombard Odier's "+230 years" — [Pictet](https://www.pictet.com/); [Rothschild](https://www.rothschildandco.com/en/); [Lombard Odier](https://www.lombardodier.com/)
- Heritage as a dated record: Pictet's "220 years" eight-moment archive and year/address timeline; Hoare's "since 1672 … 12 generations" — [Pictet](https://www.pictet.com/); [Hoare](https://www.hoaresbank.co.uk/)
- Few nav items and full-width panels: Hoare's five items behind "MENU"; Rothschild's full-width panels with uppercase group labels — [Hoare](https://www.hoaresbank.co.uk/); [Rothschild](https://www.rothschildandco.com/en/)
- Award juries reward motion, while usability and accessibility trail (Cartier 9.00 animation vs 6.80 accessibility; Yamauchi Creativity 8.24 vs Usability 6.51) — [Cartier](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/cartier-watches-wonders-2025); [Yamauchi](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/yamauchi-no-10-family-office)
- Typography and identity-derived UI can win without WebGL (Kümmerlein HM 2025) — [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/kummerlein-lawyers-notaries)

### Inferences
- Restraint looks like this: one statement per viewport, a figure band with "as at" dates (credibility through precision), a senior-voice letter, a dated chronology, regulatory and fraud notices treated as first-class content (Rothschild "Clarification", Lombard Odier's "cannot be acquired through the website"), and a closing "begin the conversation" invitation instead of "Get started".
- Template-feel looks like this: Lombard Odier's five-slide rotating hero (competing messages), Instagram embeds and an on-page form with many fields, and the ubiquitous card carousels. Even top banks fall into it below the fold.
- For Heirline: ink-navy and ivory "document" pages ruled with brass hairlines; a register/ledger list for the process (numbered entries, not cards); one signature scroll moment for the 2-of-3 vault seal; a dated "Letter from the Partner"; and a closing CTA framed as a private introduction ("Request a private conversation"), with a discreet fraud and verification note in the footer, which matters for a Nigerian HNW audience.

### Gaps
- No direct visual inspection was possible, so the hairline, type-scale and easing recommendations are inferred, not observed. Before finalising motion specs, the designer should open Pictet, Hoare, Rothschild, Cartier W&W, Kümmerlein, Yamauchi No.10 and Haute Capital in a browser, and add Hermès, Aesop, Vacheron Constantin and A. Lange & Söhne.
