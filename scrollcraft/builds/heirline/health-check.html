What we look for

Everything a family owns, wherever it is held and in whatever name. Each item is recorded as a claim, then evidenced by a document, then verified at its source.

  1. Land

    Plots, family land and buildings. We read every title document, check it against the registry, and score the risk on each title.

  2. Shares and dividends

    Holdings at registrars, including shares bought decades ago and dividends never claimed.

  3. Bank accounts

    Current, savings and dormant accounts, including balances that may have moved to a government-held fund.

  4. Pensions and policies

    Retirement savings accounts and life policies, including those with insurers that have since merged.

  5. Private interests

    Stakes in private companies and money owed to the family, with whatever evidence exists.

  6. Abroad

    Property and accounts outside Nigeria, recorded alongside everything at home.

How a title is scored

Every land title is scored against a published, versioned set of rules. The same documents always give the same score, and every report records which rules it used. No one, and no software, adjusts a score by judgement.

What you receive

A register your family can rely on, and a clear view of what to do next.

  1. The register

    Everything found, each item marked claimed, evidenced, verified or disputed, with the evidence behind it.

  2. A title plan

    Your titles ranked by risk, with the recommended action and an estimated cost range for each. Legal work is carried out by your lawyers or by our partner law firms.

  3. Money to recover

    Dividends, balances and benefits that may be recoverable, and how a claim would be made.

The signed statement

What was checked, and what was found.

An independent chartered accountant, a member of ICAN, reviews the evidence and signs an agreed-upon procedures statement. It is not an audit opinion. The person who prepares your register never signs it.

Questions

Do you write wills or give legal advice?

No. We do not write wills, hold money, act as trustee or give legal or investment advice. Wills, probate and the regularising of titles are handled by your lawyers or by our partner law firms, using the verified register.

Who can see my family's information?

Only the people working on your engagement, and only the part their task needs. Our staff see a code name, never your family name. Every view is logged and reviewed by our data protection officer.

What do you need from us to begin?

A conversation first. After that, whatever papers you hold, and letters of authority so that we can make enquiries with registrars and banks on your behalf. We tell you exactly what we need before we ask for it.

How is the fee set?

It is fixed and agreed in writing before any work begins, based on the size and spread of what your family holds. We do not publish fees.

Can you guarantee what you will find?

No one honestly can. We tell you what we found, how sure we are of each item, and what we could not verify. Where something does not hold, we say so plainly.
