# Heirline website: creative brief

**Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.** The owner asked for the website to be remade "as the world's best website designer would", keeping the brand and logo exactly as they are and aligning with the build pack. The owner's own decisions are recorded verbatim below; everything else is an authored decision and is labelled as one.

## Owner decisions (verbatim, 11 Nov 2026)

- Scope: "Website first". Portal and console come later.
- Motion: "Extend for website". Slow, weighted, scroll-linked motion and short cinematic clips on the website only. Console and portal keep the 200 to 300ms rule.
- Assets: "Approve ~80 credits" of Higgsfield generation (stills plus two clips).
- Brand: "the brand and our logo must remain as it". Ink, ivory and brass; Cormorant Garamond, Newsreader, Geist, Geist Mono; the ceremonial seal lockups unchanged; the senior-partner voice; every copy rule in CLAUDE.md §6.

## Evidence (from the build pack, not assumed)

- Audience: Nigerian HNI principals aged 50 and over, the diaspora (UK, US, UAE), executors, and partners. They use the apps of the best global private banks.
- One action: **Book a private consultation**. One label, used everywhere.
- Belief to install: *everything my family owns can be found, proven and kept safe, and Heirline cannot misuse it.*
- Hard limits: no testimonials, logo strips, pricing or unsourced statistics; no guarantees; legal work is "our partner law firms"; the vault is V1, so it is described rather than sold as live; brass only for significance; status always shown as a dot plus a label.
- Phase W scope: Home, one service page and the consultation form, at 1440px and 390px.

## The eight brief topics (authored decisions)

1. **Vibe:** *a notary's room at dusk.* Hushed, exact, permanent, warm. References: the reading room of an old private bank; a Patek Philippe catalogue; the closing scene of a land deed being stamped and sealed; Kenneth Ize's handwoven aso-oke (craft, lineage, restraint).
2. **Journey, in sequence:** a Lagos terrace as evening falls → a family's wealth scattered across places and memory → the pieces gathered into one register → land, found from above → each item proven, step by step → quiet → the register sealed under two of three keys → the four services that keep it → a private letter asking for a conversation.
3. **Energy:** calm open, a held tension in the scatter, rising through the land and the proof, near-silence, then the loudest moment of the page (the seal), then calm and generous to the end.
4. **Feeling and the one moment:** see the curve and the peak below.
5. **What no other site does:** *the visitor's own family register compiles in the margin as they read.* They mark what their family holds, watch each entry move from Claimed to Evidenced to Verified as the page explains how, and then watch it sealed. Their selection arrives in the consultation letter at the end.
6. **Distance from premium-minimal:** editorial private-house restraint (the brand requires it). Range comes from structure and the peak, not from decoration.
7. **One world or scenes:** distinct scenes. A record is compiled in entries, not travelled through. Hard changes of ground between Day (ivory) and Evening (ink), as the brand's own themes define.
8. **Assets:** existing photography (private study, land aerial, lagoon terrace at 1344px) plus a new Higgsfield set: a 4K terrace at dusk with its clean plate, a deed and survey macro, an archive room, a window-seat still life, a 4K land aerial, and two clips (evening falling on the lagoon; an aerial drift over beaconed plots). No people anywhere.

## The peak

> "The page went dark, two of three keys turned, and a brass seal pressed down on the list of everything I'd ticked."

It lives in act 7, has the largest scroll span on the page, the only Evening ground after the hero, and the silence of act 6 before it.

## Tell-someone sentence

**It's the site where you tick what your family owns, watch it get verified as you read, and then see it sealed under two of three keys.**

## Authored silence

Act 6 is intentionally near-empty: Evening ground, one line of text, roughly 0.8 of a viewport. The verification pass should treat it as silence, not dead scroll.

## Score (as built)

| Services | pan rail | 3.3 | Lateral reads as choice |
| Insights | flow | flow | Administrative: compressed |
| Close | letter (form) | flow | An input, already addressed |

Grammar: **Register** (new). The other seven lost for these reasons. Filmic one-shot and continuous world hide seams, and a register is made of entries. Live surface would mean faking the product, which is V1. Typographic poster would throw away strong photography. Gallery has no argument. Split stage has no two sides. Rhythmic cutlist is the wrong energy for this audience. Chaptered editorial came closest, but it forbids a media hero and pinned acts, and the peak needs both.

## Feel check (cold pass on the contact sheets, then diffed)

Felt: calm · curiosity · order · wonder · trust · hush · awe · warmth · resolve.
Intended: composure · unease · relief · wonder · trust · hush · awe/safety · warmth · resolve.
Diff: act 2 reads as **curiosity** more than unease. The drift is gentle and the slips invite play. That was kept on purpose: for a 50-plus principal, a calm invitation fits the brand better than manufactured anxiety, and the relief beat still lands when the pieces gather. The peak is the largest visual change and has the most scroll room. The page ends on a letter with content, not a footer.

## Privacy note for the signature move

The margin register is computed in the browser only. Nothing the visitor marks is stored or sent until they submit the consultation form themselves, and the form asks for no asset details, values or locations, only the categories they chose. If they mark nothing, the register shows an example family and says so.
